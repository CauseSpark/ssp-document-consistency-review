## 1. Purpose
This policy establishes Acme's capability to detect, respond to, and recover from
security incidents affecting the Engineering Enclave.

## 2. Policy statements

2.1 The ISSO is the incident lead and has authority to isolate affected systems.

2.2 All workforce members must report suspected incidents to the ISSO immediately.

2.3 Incidents are tracked from report to closure in TallyDesk.

2.4 Cyber incidents affecting covered defense information are reported to the DoD within
72 hours of discovery, as the contract requires.

2.5 Acme tests its incident response capability annually through a tabletop exercise.

2.6 This policy supports the incident response section of the System Security Plan
(ACME-SSP-001 v2.1).
