## 1. Purpose
This policy sets the rules for granting, using, and removing access to Acme information
systems that store or process CUI.

## 2. Scope
All workforce members, contractors, and systems in the Engineering Enclave.

## 3. Policy statements

3.1 Access is granted only after a documented request is approved by the user's manager
and the ISSO.

3.2 Each user has a unique account. Shared accounts are prohibited.

3.3 Users receive the minimum access needed for their job duties.

3.4 Privileged accounts must be separate from daily-use accounts and may only be used for
administrative tasks.

3.5 IT reviews privileged accounts annually to confirm each one is still needed.

3.6 Accounts are disabled within one business day of a workforce member's separation.

## 4. Enforcement
Violations may result in loss of access and disciplinary action.
